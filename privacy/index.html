1. Who we are

PayBlocx is a multi-currency wallet operated by SafeScope Solution Limited, a company registered in Nigeria on 19 December 2024 (registration no. 8145981), with its registered office at [registered office address]. In this policy, "PayBlocx", "we", "us" and "our" mean SafeScope Solution Limited.

We are the data controller for the personal data described here. This policy explains what we collect when you use the PayBlocx app, this website or our waitlist, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, for users in Ghana, the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, date of birth, country, your PayBlocx tag and any referral code.
  • Sign-in credentials: your password and 4-digit transaction PIN. We store these only in hashed form and can never see them.
  • Identity verification: your BVN, NIN or other government ID number, photos of your ID document, selfie and liveness images, and proof of address such as a utility bill or bank statement.
  • Payment details: bank account numbers, account names, mobile money numbers and the beneficiaries you save.
  • Support and waitlist: messages you send us, and the phone number or email you give when you join the waitlist or newsletter.

Information created when you use PayBlocx

  • Transactions: deposits, withdrawals, swaps, transfers, balances, exchange rates and fees, and your stablecoin deposit addresses.
  • Device and security data: device ID, device name and model, operating system, app version, IP address, sign-in times and active sessions, used to protect your account and alert you to new sign-ins.
  • Notifications and diagnostics: your push notification token and crash reports that help us fix problems.

If you turn on Face ID or fingerprint sign-in, the biometric check happens on your phone. We never receive your fingerprint or face data.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the service (performing our contract with you): open and run your wallets, process deposits, swaps, transfers and withdrawals, and show your history and receipts.
  • To meet legal obligations: verify your identity, apply transaction limits, prevent fraud, money laundering and terrorist financing, and respond to lawful requests from regulators and law enforcement.
  • To keep your account secure (legitimate interests): detect unusual sign-ins, manage your devices and investigate suspicious activity.
  • To communicate with you: send transaction alerts, security notices and support replies, and tell you about launch when you join the waitlist.
  • To improve PayBlocx (legitimate interests): fix bugs and understand how features are used.
  • Marketing: only with your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where it is necessary to provide PayBlocx, keep it safe or comply with the law:

  • Fincra, our licensed payment partner, to issue virtual account numbers, receive deposits and send payouts to banks and mobile money wallets.
  • Digital asset infrastructure providers that create and monitor your USDT and USDC deposit addresses.
  • Identity verification providers that check your ID number, documents and selfie.
  • Banks and mobile money operators that receive or send the payments you make.
  • Technology providers such as Google Firebase (push notifications and crash reports), hosting, email and SMS providers, acting on our instructions.
  • Regulators, law enforcement and courts where the law requires it, including the Central Bank of Nigeria and financial intelligence authorities.
  • Professional advisers such as auditors and lawyers, under confidentiality obligations.

Some of these providers store or process data outside Nigeria and Ghana. When that happens we rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as contractual protections, so your data stays protected.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it. When you close your account, or when your waitlist or newsletter entry is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise data that we are not required to keep within 30 days.

Some records must be kept longer by law. Identity verification and transaction records, for example, are kept for the period required under Nigeria's anti-money-laundering rules (currently at least five years after our relationship ends) and are then deleted.

6. How we protect it

  • Data is encrypted in transit between the app, this website and our servers.
  • Passwords and PINs are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Every payment needs your transaction PIN or biometrics, and you can review and sign out other devices.
  • Access to personal data inside PayBlocx is limited to staff who need it for their role.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have data deleted where we no longer need it or have no legal obligation to keep it;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent you have given, without affecting earlier processing.

To use these rights, email support@payblocx.com from the address on your account. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within the time the law allows. You can also delete your account from Settings → Security in the app once your balances are zero.

If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or, in Ghana, the Data Protection Commission.

8. Children

PayBlocx is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Cookies and this website

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our heading font is loaded from Fontshare, which receives your IP address in order to deliver it. When you join the waitlist or newsletter, we receive only the phone number or email you type in.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PayBlocx grows or the law changes. We will post the new version here with a new "last updated" date and, for significant changes, notify you in the app or by email.
