What is PayBlocx?

PayBlocx is a multi-currency wallet for Nigeria and Ghana. Hold Naira, Cedis, USDT and USDC, swap between them at locked rates, and send money to banks, mobile money wallets or other PayBlocx users by @tag.

Which currencies can I hold?

Nigerian Naira (NGN), Ghana Cedi (GHS), Tether (USDT) and USD Coin (USDC). Each wallet unlocks as you complete the matching verification step.

How are your exchange rates set?

Our rates are pegged at official exchange rates, so you get the best rate without parallel-market markups. Every swap shows a quote that's locked for a short time before you confirm.

How do I verify my identity?

Verification happens inside the app. Start with your basic ID details, then add documents to move up a tier. Each tier unlocks more currencies and higher daily limits.

How long do transfers take?

Transfers to other PayBlocx users by @tag are instant. Bank and mobile money payouts are usually completed within minutes, and you can track the status of each one in the app.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Any fee is shown before you confirm a transaction, together with the total amount that will leave your wallet.
