Account

Delete your PayBlocx account

You can close your account at any time. The quickest way is inside the app. If you can't sign in, send us a request below and we'll handle it for you.

Delete in the app

  1. Open PayBlocx and sign in.
  2. Tap More, then Security.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Confirm with your transaction PIN.

Before you delete

  • Withdraw any remaining balance in your Naira, Cedi and stablecoin wallets. We can't send funds once an account is closed.
  • Wait for any pending transfers, swaps or withdrawals to complete.
  • Deletion is permanent. You'll need to sign up and verify again to use PayBlocx later.

What happens to your data

  • Deleted: your profile, login details, saved bank accounts and beneficiaries, app preferences and device data, within 30 days of your request.
  • Kept for a limited time: transaction records and identity verification (KYC) details that financial and anti-money-laundering laws require us to keep, usually for at least 5 years. These are stored securely, used only to meet those legal duties, and then deleted.

Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Request deletion

Use the email or phone number registered on your PayBlocx account. We'll confirm the request with you before your account is closed.

Never include your PIN, password or one-time codes. We will never ask for them.
